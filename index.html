About

Liang Zhou is a professor and vice president at Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications. He received the B.S. and M.S. degrees from Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications in 2003 and 2006, respectively, and the Ph.D. degree from Université Paris-Saclay, France, and Shanghai Jiao Tong University, China, in 2009. He then conducted postdoctoral research at ParisTech, France, and the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, USA. From 2010 to 2011, as a Humboldt Research Fellow, he worked at the Technical University of Munich, Germany. His honors include Changjiang Distinguished Professor of the Ministry of Education (2020), the National Science Fund for Excellent Young Scholars (2013), IEEE Fellow, and the Humboldt Research Award, among others.

His research focuses on communications engineering and artificial intelligence. In recent years, he has published papers in journals such as Nature Sensors, Nature Communications, and IEEE JSAC. He has led key projects of the National Natural Science Foundation of China and industry–academia collaboration projects with companies including Huawei and ZTE. He currently serves as Chair of the IEEE Communications Society Multimedia Communications Technical Committee, Director of a professional committee of the China Institute of Communications, and as an editorial board member of journals including IEEE Transactions on Communications and IEEE Wireless Communications.

Research Interests

Core themes spanning wireless, multimedia, and intelligent communications.

Wireless Communications

Resource allocation, scheduling, D2D, and next-generation networking for reliable connectivity.

Multimedia Communications

Video streaming, QoE-driven design, media cloud, and large-scale multimedia delivery.

Intelligent Communications

Cross-modal and semantic communication, immersive multi-sensory systems, and AI-enabled networking.

IoT & Smart Systems

Internet of Things architectures, industrial IoT, and smart-city multimedia services.
